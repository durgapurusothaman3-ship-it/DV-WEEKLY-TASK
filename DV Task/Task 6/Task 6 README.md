# Week 6 – Healthcare Data Visualization, Cost Relationship & Policy Insights

## Overview

This project focuses on analyzing healthcare data using data visualization and statistical techniques. The analysis explores billing amounts, medical conditions, insurance providers, patient admission trends, and relationships between age, hospital stay duration, and billing amount.

## Objectives

- Compare billing amounts across medical conditions and insurance providers.
- Create stacked bar charts and violin plots for healthcare cost analysis.
- Analyze patient admission trends over time.
- Identify seasonal or high-admission periods.
- Generate a correlation matrix for age, stay duration, and billing amount.
- Understand relationships between healthcare variables.
- Provide healthcare management recommendations based on the analysis.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Healthcare Dataset

The dataset contains patient information such as:

- Age
- Gender
- Medical Condition
- Date of Admission
- Discharge Date
- Insurance Provider
- Billing Amount
- Admission Type
- Medication
- Test Results

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Billing Analysis
A stacked bar chart was created to compare total billing amounts across different medical conditions and insurance providers.

### 2. Billing Distribution
Violin plots were used to understand the distribution and variation of billing amounts across medical conditions.

### 3. Admission Trend Analysis
A temporal line chart was created to analyze monthly patient admissions and identify periods of increased or decreased admissions.

### 4. Correlation Analysis
A correlation matrix was generated using:

- Age
- Stay Duration
- Billing Amount

The analysis showed that these variables have very weak linear relationships with each other.

### 5. Healthcare Insights
The analysis helps healthcare management understand patient admission patterns, billing variations, and cost relationships.

## Key Findings

- Billing amounts vary across medical conditions and insurance providers.
- Patient admissions change over time, helping identify high-admission periods.
- Age, hospital stay duration, and billing amount show very weak linear correlation.
- Additional factors such as treatment, medication, tests, and procedures may be needed for better cost analysis.

## Healthcare Management Recommendations

- Improve staff and bed allocation during high-admission periods.
- Monitor billing amounts across medical conditions and insurance providers.
- Consider multiple factors when analyzing healthcare costs.
- Use admission trends for better resource planning.
- Regularly monitor healthcare billing for effective financial management.

## Conclusion

This project provides a visual and statistical understanding of healthcare data. The analysis of billing, admissions, and correlations can help healthcare organizations improve resource planning, cost monitoring, and management decisions.
